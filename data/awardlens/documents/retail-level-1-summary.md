# Supported calculation scope

AwardLens calculates synthetic examples for adult Retail Employee Level 1 ordinary hours only. It supports full-time, part-time, and casual records on weekdays, weekday evenings after 6 pm, Saturdays, Sundays, and explicitly marked public holidays.

The verified minimum adult Level 1 hourly rate effective 1 July 2026 is $27.81. For full-time and part-time ordinary hours, the supported multipliers are 100% on weekdays, 125% after 6 pm Monday to Friday and on Saturday, 150% on Sunday, and 225% on a public holiday. For casual ordinary hours, the inclusive multipliers are 125%, 150%, 150%, 175%, and 250% respectively.

# Precedence and exclusions

An explicitly marked public holiday takes precedence over the weekday or weekend. AwardLens does not calculate juniors, apprentices, trainees, overtime, shiftworkers, allowances, overnight work, enterprise agreements, coverage questions, or a shift that crosses a penalty-rate boundary. Those records receive manual review without an expected amount or liability.

# Safety notice

The data is synthetic and the output is an educational comparison, not legal or payroll advice. AwardLens does not determine coverage and does not conclude that any person or organisation has breached a law.

Official references: Fair Work Commission award MA000004 and the Fair Work Ombudsman pay guide effective 1 July 2026.
