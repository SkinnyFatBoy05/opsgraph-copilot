# Transaction Monitoring Review Guide

> Portfolio disclaimer: This is a fictional operational guide for synthetic records, not a real financial-crime control.

## Flag review

Every flagged transaction must be assigned to Financial Crime Operations. The reviewer must inspect the transaction amount, direction, category, account status, customer risk rating, and any related flagged activity.

## Priority

A flagged transaction belonging to a high-risk customer or a restricted account is priority work. Priority reviews must be acknowledged within four business hours. Other flagged transactions must be acknowledged within one business day.

## Permitted copilot behavior

The copilot may retrieve the synthetic guide, query read-only demo records, and explain which facts caused a case to be selected. It may not clear a flag, restrict an account, file a report, or execute any other real-world action.

## Audit evidence

The completed review must preserve the record identifiers, query time, policy version, reviewer, decision, and supporting notes.
